Sources & licences

Image credits

This page documents the sources, creators and licence information for external image assets used in Wahltrends content.

Individual entries are currently being reconciled with the internal licence register and will be completed before this page is used as a publication credit reference.

Edited image assets

Source images may be cropped, isolated, resized, desaturated or colour-adjusted for the Wahltrends visual design. Source, creator and licence information always refer to the underlying original work. Where a licence requires modification notices or ShareAlike distribution, this is reflected in the corresponding credit entry.
